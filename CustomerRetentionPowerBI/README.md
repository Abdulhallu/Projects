These Power BI dashboards explore customer churn, risk segmentation, and service usage insights using predictive visualizations.

### Contents:
- **Customer Churn Dashboard**: Highlights churn demographics, payment methods, contract types, and service subscriptions.
- **Customer Risk Analysis Dashboard**: Visualizes churn rates by contract, internet service, and payment behavior, enabling proactive retention efforts.
